# Human footage: sources and licences

Live-action stock footage for the two Kairos films (product and technical). Clips are not committed: they're large, and the licences don't allow redistributing unaltered copies. To re-download, run `sh fetch_human.sh`. Contact sheets (6 frames per clip, with timecodes) are in `sheets/H<n>.jpg`.

- **Pixabay Content License** (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/): free for commercial use, no attribution required. The same restriction on reselling unaltered clips applies. One clip (H2_5).
- None of the clips has a watermark. Sources not used: YouTube, paid libraries, and Mixkit/Coverr, which had nothing on-brief for these shots.

`In/out` is the best usable range in seconds. **Bold** marks the first pick for each shot.

## Casting continuity (same person across clips)
- **Asha (ops lead):** H5_1–H5_4 are one actress in one shoot (Yan Krukau): an office with bookshelves, a white shirt and a silver laptop.
- **Support agent:** H10_1–H10_4 are one actress in one shoot (Yan Krukau, a call centre). She isn't the same woman as Asha, but it's the same photographer and the same grade. I didn't find a free series where one actress appears both at a laptop and in a headset.
- **Vikram (borrower):**
  - H2_4 (annoyed on the phone, bedroom) and H7_6 (smiling at a laptop with a mug) are the same Delhi actor, in the same maroon T-shirt and the same room. That's the best same-person pair.
  - H7_1–H7_4 are one Sikh man at home (Ketut Subiyanto): he reads a text seriously, then smiles.
  - H2_1–H2_2 are one man outdoors with a helmet (Mumbai sea-front).
  - No outdoor clip shares a face with any home clip.
- **Second borrower (woman, "Sunita"):** H4_3 and H4_4 are the same Indian woman at home (same Ketut Subiyanto shoot as H7_1–H7_4), but she's smiling. H4_1 is a strong worried close-up of a different woman, in a studio. H4_2 is an older woman at her shop doorway, with no phone.
