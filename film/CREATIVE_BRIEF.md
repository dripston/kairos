# Creative Brief: Kairos (CreditNirvana Agentathon film, unofficial team film)

**Format:** full film 3:36 (216 s) + operator cut 1:29 (89 s) | 16:9 master, 9:16 plan in SHOT_LIST.md | hybrid: code-rendered motion graphics + real product screens | 30 fps
**Audience / use case:** hackathon judges, the room, a lender's collections-operations head; laypeople, no jargon.
**Big idea (one sentence):** Collections stop being a calendar and become a decision: Kairos decides, every day for every borrower, whether to reach out, how, and when, and the operator just watches the score.
**Human insight / friction:** borrowers drown in identical reminders and stop reading; lenders pay for every ignored message; ops teams babysit schedules.
**Primary emotion:** relief (for Ravi and for Priya), with a little fun (cricket).
**Single promise:** Right message. Right time. Or none at all.
**Desired takeaway:** "It decides for each person, it's safe, and it brings back about twice the money per rupee."
**One visual signature:** the phone screen that grows into the real CRM record (shared-element morph), and the cricket scoreboard where rupees are runs and wickets (rule breaches) stay at zero.
**Brand voice / palette / typography:** calm, warm, plain English with a little Hinglish; navy #06101d stage, teal #2dd4bf accent, amber/violet sparingly; system sans (Inter / SF), large type.
**Product references and usage rights:** real Kairos screens captured from our own repo (`origin/main`), synthetic data only. CreditNirvana and Sarvam named as plain text where factual; no third-party logos; chat UI is generic (no WhatsApp trade dress). Music and foley are original, generated by `music/compose.py`.
**CTA / final card:** "Kairos. Right message. Right time. Or none at all." (full) · "Kairos. Collections on autopilot. You just watch the score." (operator) + The A Team · CreditNirvana Agentathon 2026.

**Storytelling mode:** hybrid HUMAN_STORY (Ravi the borrower, Priya the operator) + FEATURE_EXPLAINER (each feature: use → close-up → result). Explanations are spoken and captioned; nothing important is montage-only.

## Production assumptions
- Budget & assets: zero budget; everything rendered locally (HTML/CSS/JS, Python stdlib audio, macOS `say` draft voices, headless Chrome captures).
- Constraints: no Node on this Mac, so the React demo UI was not captured; `/demo/live` is not on main, so the scoreboard is a high-fidelity rebuild in the product's design language.
- Music source/licensing: original, generated by `music/compose.py` (see `music/LICENSE.md`).
- Places where official footage / UI is required: Control Room and live scoreboard captures once available (SHOT_LIST.md).

## Creative review
- Explains something observable by 10–15 s? Partly: the problem is clear by 0:10 and the brand mark is on screen from 0:00; the product is named at 0:19.8 and shown deciding at 0:24.
- Shows product utility at least twice? Yes: the borrower journey (s4 + real CRM) and the operator's day (o1–o4), plus real CM-03 screens.
- Honest and legally usable? Yes: SIMULATED labels, qualifiers in the footnote, original music, generic chat UI, labelled unofficial team film.
- Original vs. stylistic imitation? Original structure (borrower → operator → proof); the cricket scoreboard is our own device; no copied ad structure, copy or soundtrack.
