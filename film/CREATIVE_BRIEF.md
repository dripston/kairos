# Creative Brief: Kairos (CreditNirvana Agentathon film, unofficial team film)

**Format:** one film, 2:19.6 (139.635 s), 16:9, 1920×1080 stage. Real footage + device renders with real product screens + kinetic type.
**Audience:** hackathon judges, the room, a lender's collections head; laypeople, no jargon.
**Big idea:** collections stop being a calendar and become a decision. For every borrower, every day, Kairos decides whether to reach out, how and when, and Aladdin makes sure it is allowed.
**Human insight:** borrowers get the same reminder at the same time as everyone else and stop reading; lenders pay for every ignored message.
**Primary emotion:** calm relief, then confidence at the proof.
**Single promise:** the right message, at the right time. Or none at all.
**Desired takeaway:** "It decides for each person, it's safe, and it brings back about twice the money per rupee."
**Clarity rule:** the voice is the script. Every line gets the one visual that proves it, held long enough to read.

## Look and sound

- **Look:** Apple-launch. Black spotlight stage for reveals (Meet Kairos, the proof), warm light studio for
  explanation, film grain, one muted accent (`#0F766E`). No neon, no tiles. Device renders (laptop, phone, no
  logos) always carry a real or rebuilt product screen; no empty screens. Camera moves use one launch ease.
- **Real world:** Pexels footage of Indian cities and people on phones (n01–n03, n04 hands on a laptop, n10, n17), graded to the palette.
- **Chat:** a real-looking chat for the WhatsApp agent: header with "Lender ✓ · verified business", beige
  patterned background, bubbles timed to the voice, typing dots. Generic UI, no messenger logo or name in the UI.
- **Voice:** Neha only (Sarvam bulbul:v3, pace 1.2, AI-generated). She also voices the agent's Hindi line, captioned in English.
- **Music:** original cinematic trailer score (`music/original/`), ducked 11 dB under Neha. Hits drive hard cuts, a
  2-frame flash and a 4% punch-in. Big hits: the laptop lights up at "Meet Kairos" (26.28 s) and the giant ~2× (118.09 s).
  The STOP frame holds still in the 72.9–73.7 s silence.
- **Captions:** on by default, the exact VO text, bottom centre, white on a soft shadow, two lines max.
