# Kairos Theme: licence

"Kairos Theme" and all music, transitions and UI sound effects in this folder are an original
composition generated by `compose.py` for the Kairos film, owned by **The A Team**.

- Generated entirely from code (Python standard library: `math`, `random` with a fixed seed, `wave`).
- No samples, loops, presets or recordings from any third party; no melody or arrangement is taken
  from an existing song or advertisement.
- Re-running `compose.py` reproduces the same audio from the same scene table.

The character and narrator voices (`vo_*.wav/.m4a`, and the voices inside `soundtrack_*`) are a draft
made with the macOS built-in `say` voices (Rishi, Aman, Tara, Lekha) for internal review. Check
Apple's terms before any public release, or replace them with a licensed voice (for example the
team's Sarvam TTS voices Ritu and Advait) or a human recording.
