  1. One simulated campaign: 300 synthetic borrowers, 14 days, on the real Kairos engines. Nothing is sent: mock adapters, dry-run.
  2. Left, the churn engine: every reply, payment, missed call and the daily check re-sorts borrowers into a cohort, a risk tier and a contact layer. Every move names its rule — click one.
  3. Right, the learning loop: a Bayesian learner that updates from every reply. Bar = how likely each channel gets a reply; the shade is how sure it is. It narrows as evidence comes in.
  4. Day 7: we change the world. WhatsApp replies drop to about a third. Kairos is not told — watch the WhatsApp estimates fall from day 8.
  5. The learner shifts 1–30 days late towards SMS as soon as SMS brings more replies per rupee. Aladdin checks every send; cap breaches stay at 0.
