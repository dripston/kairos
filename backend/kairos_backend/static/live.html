Rules Aladdin checks

  • Quiet days and hoursNo contact on Sundays or outside the allowed hours.
  • Daily and weekly limitsA cap on messages per person, per channel and per week, set by the lender.
  • Consent and STOPOnly channels the borrower agreed to. After a STOP, every channel goes quiet.
  • BudgetSpending is paced across the campaign. When today's budget is used up, the rest wait.
  • Holds and approvalsPromises, hardship and disputed payments pause contact until a person decides.
